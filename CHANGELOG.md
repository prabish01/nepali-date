# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Unreleased

- Immutable `BSDate`, pure AD/BS conversion and explicit supported ranges.
- Pinned BS 1975–2083 calendar data with provenance, licensing and checksums.
- Strict English/Nepali parsing, formatting, weekdays and numeral conversion.
- Calendar arithmetic, comparisons, day intervals, month grids and bounds.
- IANA timezone-safe instant conversion with historical Nepal offset handling.
- Nepal fiscal-year labels, quarters and complete boundary helpers.
- 45 dated MOHA 2083 holiday records with explicit eligibility scopes.
- Tithi names, supplied-longitude classification and sourced interval lookup.
- ESM/CommonJS entries, TypeScript declarations, browser-bundler checks,
  exhaustive date tests, package-install smoke checks and timezone CI.

No npm release has been published. No daily ephemeris/tithi almanac is bundled;
unannounced holiday dates and later/local holiday notices remain out of scope.
