Clear about the details.

A useful calendar library starts with knowing exactly what its data covers.

A real, finite calendar.

BS 1975–2083, backed by a versioned month-length table. Dates beyond the table throw instead of guessing.

Calendar provenance

Nepal time, correctly.

IANA timezone rules handle modern UTC+05:45 and Nepal’s historical offsets. Civil dates stay separate from instants.

Sources before assumptions.

Holidays carry sources and eligibility scopes. Tithi accepts supplied longitudes or sourced intervals; a daily almanac isn’t bundled.
