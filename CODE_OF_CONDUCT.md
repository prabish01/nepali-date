# Community conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Explain disagreements using evidence and
reproducible examples. Welcome people with different experience levels, languages
and backgrounds. Critique the work without attacking the person.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted personal contact and
publication of private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or
remove harmful contributions, limit participation or close discussions to keep
the project constructive. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer
using an available contact channel on their profile.
